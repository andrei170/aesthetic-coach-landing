The honest part

Being qualified is not the same as being booked.

You spent thousands learning to do the treatments, and not a penny on the business behind them. So the skill is there, but the calendar is half empty, the prices feel stuck, and the next client is always a question mark. That gap, between qualified and fully booked, is where most practitioners get trapped. It is not your hands. It is the business.

Your calendar is unpredictable: busy one week, quiet the next, never steady.
You undercharge because someone local is always cheaper and you are scared to lose the booking.
Clients come from luck and word of mouth, with no system bringing them in.
You post on Instagram constantly and it does not turn into bookings.
You watch other injectors get booked out and cannot see what they do differently.
You are brilliant in the treatment room and lost the moment it becomes a business.
